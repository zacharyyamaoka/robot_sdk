robot-sdk: 13 public repos, ready for PyPI

Every package in the SDK is public on GitHub, pinned in robot-sdk, scanned and wheel-tested. Nothing is on PyPI yet beyond the two that already were: publishing waits on one sign-in step only you can do (11 PyPI pending publishers), then tags go out in dependency order.
